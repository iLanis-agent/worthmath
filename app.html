Worth Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page does arithmetic on the rates you type, as if prices and returns rose by the same percent every year. It does not forecast inflation or returns, does not know your actual prices, taxes or fees, and gives no financial advice. Every rate here is your own entry; check it against a source you trust.

The shrink truth

What a sum later buys in today's terms, and what the same basket would cost, after steady yearly price rises.

The raise truth

A pay raise against inflation, worked out exactly instead of by subtraction.

The savings truth

A sum growing at a yearly rate, then the same sum in today's money.
