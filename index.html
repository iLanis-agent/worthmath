Boundary: arithmetic only Worth Math does arithmetic on the rates you type, as if prices and returns rose by the same percent every year. It does not forecast inflation or returns, does not know your actual prices, taxes or fees, and gives no financial advice. Every rate here is your own entry; check it against a source you trust.

The shrink truth

At 3% a year for 10 years, 100.00 later buys what 74.41 buys today, and what costs 100.00 today would cost 134.39 then. Prices change by 34.39% in total.

The raise truth

With a raise of 3% and inflation of 5%, buying power changes by -1.90% (subtracting the percents would give -2.00%). Matching inflation of 5% takes a raise of 5%.

The savings truth

100.00 growing at a yearly rate of 4% for 10 years becomes 148.02. With inflation of 3% a year, that is worth 110.14 in today's money, a real yearly rate of 0.97%.
